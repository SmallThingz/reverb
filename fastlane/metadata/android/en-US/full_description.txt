Reverb lets you capture audio, keep a rolling history and save the parts you need.

<b>Capture</b>
<ul>
<li>Independent One-shot and Looping buffers</li>
<li>Disk-backed audio history that restores across app restarts</li>
<li>Configurable retention limits by time or size</li>
<li>Hardware-aware audio format, sample rate, source, route and channel options</li>
</ul>

<b>Save and listen</b>
<ul>
<li>Select a range on the waveform and export it as WAV</li>
<li>Listen while fine-tuning your selection</li>
<li>Play saved recordings directly in the Library</li>
<li>Trim into a new recording while preserving the original</li>
<li>Rename, move and delete saved recordings</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, trackers or accounts. Reverb does not request network permission.

<b>Battery use</b>
Continuous microphone capture consumes power even though Reverb reduces work
when its capture screen is not visible. Battery use varies by device and
recording settings.
