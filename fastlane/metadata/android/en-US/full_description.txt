Reverb keeps a rolling audio buffer on your Android device so you can save
something that has just happened. Audio that you do not save is automatically
discarded as the buffer rolls forward.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Disk-backed rolling buffer that survives app restarts</li>
<li>Streamed WAV export without loading the full recording into memory</li>
<li>Built-in playback, rename, delete, and move tools</li>
<li>Configurable retention time and storage limits</li>
<li>Hardware-aware audio format, sample rate, source, route, and channel options</li>
<li>No ads, trackers, accounts, or network access</li>
</ul>

<b>Battery use</b>

Continuous microphone capture consumes power even though Reverb reduces work
when its capture screen is not visible. Battery impact varies by device and is
similar to continuous audio playback.
